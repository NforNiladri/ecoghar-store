Why we built PureTap, and how to check your tap water

Many Kolkata homes depend on an RO service or a heavy purifier just to get clean drinking water. PureTap is a simpler option: it clips onto your kitchen tap and filters water as it flows.

Inside is a ceramic and carbon filter. A digital TDS display on the unit lets you see a reading instead of guessing. It comes with 6 universal adapters, so it fits most taps.

Before you buy anything, check your own water. Borewell and municipal water in North Dumdum can differ from street to street, so a quick TDS reading tells you where you start.

Replace the cartridge every 3 to 4 months. Scan the QR on the box to reorder in one tap, with 15% off for repeat customers.

A kitchen composter that doesn’t smell

Wet kitchen waste is the part of the bin that smells first and attracts fruit flies. BioBin keeps it out of sight and out of the air.

The drum is food-grade and airtight, and the lid has a carbon filter that handles odour. A small tap at the bottom lets you drain liquid fertiliser for your plants.

Add your scraps, sprinkle in the compost maker powder, and close the lid. A 1 kg pack of powder costs ₹249, or ₹210 when you reorder through the QR on your box.

It’s a good fit for apartments, because it lives on your counter and doesn’t need a garden.
